Your donation turns into real veteran impact.

Every dollar pushes a veteran build forward — from components and shipping to the setup that makes the computer usable on day one.

06Transparency

No smoke.No guessing.Just visible impact.

Supporters deserve to know where their money goes, what it supports, and how each build reaches a real veteran. AGB was built on trust, accountability, and community-visible impact — so the books are public. Most charities show a pie chart. AGB links to the real transaction feed.

07Why it matters

One build can change the direction of a life.

For some veterans, a computer is the first step back into school. For others it opens the door to work, gaming, streaming, telehealth — or simply having people to talk to again.

One machine, delivered free, is rarely just a machine. It is the thing that makes the next step possible. Named veteran stories run here once the veteran who gave one has signed off on every word.
