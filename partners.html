Corporate Partners & Sponsors

Put your brand behind veteran impact people can actually see.

AGB partners with companies that want their charitable investment connected to something tangible. A partnership can fund a veteran PC build, expand a technology program, support community access, provide equipment, sponsor an event, increase technical support capacity, or help take a proven program into a new market. We work with partners to create a clear connection between what you contribute and what that contribution makes possible.

01Partnership is more than a logo

We value visibility. The real value is the impact behind it.

Corporate partners can receive program specific impact reporting, approved recognition opportunities, event integration, community engagement, content opportunities, employee involvement, and stories demonstrating what the partnership helped accomplish.

Veteran privacy and dignity always come first. Storytelling is done with consent.
