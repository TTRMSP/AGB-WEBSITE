Veteran PC Program

You served. This is one of the ways we give back.

Always Give Back provides custom built computers to qualifying disabled U.S. military veterans at no cost to the recipient. This application helps our team understand the applicant's circumstances, technology needs, intended use, and how a computer could support their next step.

Submitting an application does not guarantee selection. Available systems are dependent on program funding, eligibility, current demand, and available resources.

We treat every applicant with dignity and respect, and information submitted through this application is used to evaluate participation in the program.
