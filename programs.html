Programs & Funding Priorities

The model is already built. Funding helps us scale it.

AGB's next stage is not about adding dozens of unrelated programs. It is about taking the programs we already operate and bringing them to more veterans, more communities, and more partner organizations. AGB focuses its resources on practical technology programs where funding can be connected directly to action and measurable results.

01The build programme

Veteran PC Program

Modern education, employment, communication, telehealth, entertainment, and social connection increasingly require capable technology.

What AGB does

We provide qualifying disabled veterans with custom built computer systems at no cost to the recipient.

Funding supports

Hardware, peripherals, shipping, setup, deployment, warranty support, technical assistance, and program expansion.

We measure

Applications received, systems completed, veterans served, geographic reach, program costs, and continued technical support.

02Digital independence

Tech Literacy Hub

Access to technology does not automatically create digital independence.

What AGB does

We help veterans develop the practical technology skills needed to confidently use computers, email, internet services, telehealth tools, online resources, and modern communication platforms.

Funding supports

Computers, internet access, training materials, technical support, community locations, program development, and expansion.

We measure

Veterans assisted, training engagements, participating locations, support requests, and program growth.

Need help right now? Veterans can get live technical support 24/7 in the AGB Tech Hub Discord — real people, real time, no appointment.

03Removing the cost barrier

Free Tech Support

Technology problems can quickly become barriers to work, school, communication, and access to services.

What AGB does

We provide accessible technical assistance designed to help people solve real technology problems without the cost becoming another barrier.

Funding supports

Staffing capacity, support infrastructure, software, equipment, outreach, and expansion.

We measure

People assisted, issues resolved, support volume, and service reach.

04More than a device

Community Connection

Technology is most valuable when it connects people rather than simply giving them another device.

What AGB does

AGB connects veterans with an online community built around technology, gaming, shared interests, events, and mutual support.

Funding supports

Community technology, events, outreach, program infrastructure, and veteran participation.
